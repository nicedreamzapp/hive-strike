## Full description

You are the last bee out of a burning hive.

Sixteen worlds stand between you and the queen who took it: a sunlit meadow, a moonlit
pine wood, the inside of the comb itself, a volcano floor, a rooftop after dusk, a
crystal cavern lit only by its own stones. Each one is a painting, and each one has a
bug in it that a camera would recognise.

Fly with one finger. The bee follows your thumb, so both hands stay out of the way of
the screen. Sixteen bosses change their job halfway through the fight rather than just
getting angrier: the centipede mother splits in two and pinches, the scorpion plants
its tail as a turret and flanks you with its body, the atlas moth turns its wings into
shields you have to break.

Sixteen guns, each with five levels and a look of its own. A honey fan for a crowd, a
stinger beam for one thing you want gone, seeker pollen that never misses, a resin wall
you hide behind, a swarm of drones that hunt for you.

Forty-eight bugs, and a Bug-Dex that fills in as you beat them, each card carrying one
true fact about the real animal.

**One payment.** Play the whole game free for thirty days. After that, $1.99 unlocks it
forever, on every device signed in to your account. There is no subscription, nothing
else to buy, and no adverts anywhere in it.

- Sixteen hand-painted worlds and sixteen bosses
- Sixteen weapons, five levels each
- A Bug-Dex of forty-eight real insects
- Daily runs and eight contracts that change the rules
- One thumb, portrait, no tutorial to sit through
- Plays offline
