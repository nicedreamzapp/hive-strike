Hive Strike — Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 September 2026

Hive Strike does not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no trackers of any kind. The game does not ask for your name, your email address, your location, your contacts, your photos, your microphone, or your camera, because it does not use any of them.

What the game stores, and where

Everything the game remembers is stored on your own device and never leaves it:

  • Your high score, best score per world, and which worlds you have unlocked
  • Which bugs you have beaten, for the Bug-Dex
  • Your music and sound settings
  • Your saved place, if you quit part way through a run
  • The date you first opened the game, which is how the free month is counted
  • Whether the full unlock has been purchased

Deleting the app removes all of it. None of it is sent anywhere.

The purchase

Hive Strike is free for thirty days. After that, a single payment of $1.99 unlocks it forever. That payment is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or by Google Play. We never see your card, your billing address, or your account details. The store tells the app one thing only: whether the unlock has been bought. Apple and Google handle that transaction under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Network use

The game plays offline. It contacts the network only when you tap the unlock or the restore button, and then only to reach the App Store or Google Play.

Children

The game is suitable for children and collects nothing from anyone, of any age. It has no chat, no user content, no links out except this policy, and no advertising.
