## Centipede head — what was actually wrong (22:33)
The verifier was right that it was an art fault, not draw code. Two things:
- `centihead1.png` was cut from `centiseg.png`, whose white studio background was only partly
  keyed. A soft cream blob survived behind the head with a hard edge — that is the "head detaches"
  Matt kept seeing. The key now cuts on brightness AND low saturation with a ramp, then drops the
  remaining fringe, so nothing pale survives against the dark body.
- The face genuinely was a beetle. `centihead` has been regenerated asking for a flat wedge head
  plate, long forward antennae and curved forcipules; both tiles are now cut from that one render,
  so head and body match in colour. Segment band comes from the upper body, which also drops the
  repeating stamped artifact the verifier spotted in the old tile.


## Halo fix, final (22:44)
The halo was NOT white — sampling it gave rgb (160,167,167) at alpha 195, a neutral grey drop
shadow, which is why every brightness threshold missed it. Alpha now comes from `saturated OR
dark`: the centipede is either strongly coloured or nearly black, the halo is desaturated
mid-grey. Then unpremultiply so edge pixels are not milky. This is now `key_white` in
tools/gen_sprites.py, so every future sprite gets it, not just the centipede tiles.
